🚪 How to Create a Room

Start a listening session as a Host in just two clicks.

Topic 1 / 7
1
Display Name
2
Room Name
3

  • 1 Enter Your Display Name

    Type your name (e.g., "Sam") so friends know who is hosting.

  • 2 Name Your Room

    Choose a session name (e.g., "Music Lounge" or "Study Beats").

  • 3 Click "+ Create private room"

    Your private room is generated instantly with an 8-character code.

💡 Host Privilege As host, you can share tab audio, approve listener audio requests, and lock or end the room.

🔗 How to Join an Existing Room

Connect with a room code or link shared by a friend.

  • 1 Paste the Room Code

    Enter the 8-character code (e.g. Y8V4D67G). Hyphens & lowercase are automatically cleaned.

    Tell the room who joined the party.

  • 3 Click "Enter room"

    You will immediately sync to the live high-fidelity audio stream!

💡 Direct Invite Links If your friend sent you a link like ?room=ABCDEF12, the code is filled automatically!

🎵 How to Share Audio (Real Interface)

Broadcast high-fidelity browser audio or audio files directly to all participants.

Topic 3 / 7
1
Share Tab Audio
2
Play MP3/WAV/FLAC
3
Visual Cassette Deck
  • 1 Click "Share audio"

    A browser picker appears. Select your Chrome/Edge tab (e.g. YouTube, Spotify Web, SoundCloud) and check "Also share tab audio".

  • 2 Or "Share audio file"

    Want to play a local song? Click to pick any MP3, WAV, AAC, or FLAC track directly from your computer.

  • 3 Watch the Cassette Spin

    The retro cassette reels spin in real time and the visualizer syncs with the audio rhythm.

💡 Pro Sound Quality SangeetPitara uses lossless PCM16 WebSockets + WebRTC Opus relay, bypassing microphone noise filters so music sounds rich and punchy.
