Creating Room

Step-by-step procedure and real-time environment for launching a new listening session.

Step-by-Step Guide
Real-time SangeetPitara Room Creation Screen showing Display Name, Room Name, and "+ Create private room".

Procedure Details

Step 1: Enter Your Display Name

Type your name (e.g., "Sam") in the first field so other listeners know who is hosting.

Step 2: Enter Room Title

Name your room (e.g., "Music Lounge", "Study Beats", or "Midnight Acoustic").

Step 3: Click "+ Create private room"

Your private room is generated immediately with an unambiguous 8-character code, and you enter as the Host.

💡 Host Privileges As Host, you have exclusive control to broadcast tab/device audio, review listener requests, assign Co-hosts, lock the room, or end the session.

Joining Room

Connect with a room code or an invite URL to listen to synchronized audio instantly.

Step-by-Step Guide
Real-time SangeetPitara Join Screen showing Room Code field, Display Name, and "→ Join room" button.

Procedure Details

Step 1: Get the 8-Character Room Code

Ask the room Host for their 8-character room code (e.g., 3HLYQKQT) or click their invite link.

Step 2: Enter Your Display Name

Type the name you want listeners in the room to see in the chat and participants roster.

Step 3: Click "→ Join room"

You are connected to the live room immediately. Once audio begins, sound plays through your speakers or headphones in sync.

🎧 Instant Sync No user account or password is required. Just open the link and start listening!

Host Room & Audio Sharing

Broadcast music with real-time interfaces across Windows Desktop, Android Phone casting, or local audio files.

Audio Controls
Real Windows 11 Chrome share dialog with arrows pointing to the critical "Also share tab audio" checkbox.

Windows Desktop Audio Procedure

Step 1: Open SangeetPitara in Chrome or Edge

Make sure your music service (Spotify Web, YouTube, SoundCloud) is open in another browser tab.

Step 2: Click "Share tab audio" in Your Room

The native Chrome/Edge screen share dialog appears automatically.

Step 3: Check "Also share tab audio" (CRITICAL)

Select the tab playing your music, and make sure the "Also share tab audio" checkbox in the bottom left is checked!

Step 4: Click "Share"

Audio is captured in pristine fidelity and relayed instantly to every listener in the room.

Real Android Phone Interface: Tap "Share device audio" → Select Scope: "Entire screen" → Tap "Start".

Android Phone Audio Procedure

Step 1: Tap "Share device audio"

In your mobile room, tap the prominent orange "Share device audio" button.

Step 2: Choose Scope: "Entire screen" (CRITICAL)

When Android asks "Start recording or casting with SangeetPitara?", set Scope to "Entire screen" so playback from music apps is captured.

Step 3: Tap "Start"

Now switch to any music app (Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music) and press Play — your friends hear everything live!

📱 Native Android App: For background casting without keeping the screen on, install the free SangeetPitara Android APK.
Built-in Cassette Audio Visualizer with "Share audio file" button.

Local Audio File Procedure

Step 1: Click "Share audio file"

Click the audio file button directly below the cassette visualizer.

Step 2: Select Your Music File

Pick any MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, or OGG file from your device storage.

Step 3: Play & Synchronize

The retro cassette reels spin and high-fidelity audio streams in real-time to everyone in the room.

Inviting Users

Manage participants, copy instant invite links, promote Co-hosts, and secure your room.

Room Management
Real-time SangeetPitara People Panel showing Share invite link, Room Code, and Role badges.

Procedure Details

Step 1: Click "Share invite link"

Click "Share invite link" inside the room or on the People panel to copy the direct URL to your clipboard.

Step 2: Send to Friends

Paste the link into WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, or SMS. Anyone clicking the link joins immediately.

Step 3: Share the 8-Character Code

Alternatively, give friends the 8-character room code to enter on the landing page.

Step 4: Promote Co-Hosts or Lock Room

Promote trusted listeners to Co-host to share audio control, or click "Lock room" to prevent uninvited guests.

Other Feature

Explore real-time features for Chat, People roster, Help & Health diagnostics, and Studio Themes.

Features

• Floating Reactions: Tap ❤️, 🔥, 👏, 🎧 for animated real-time emojis.

• Save Diagnostics: Export anonymous debug logs.

• 5 Themes: Dark, Light, Forest, Brass, and Rose.
• Topbar Switcher: Change theme instantly.
• Black Header: High contrast on all 5 themes.
