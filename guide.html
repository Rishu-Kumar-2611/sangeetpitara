Creating Room

Step-by-step procedure and real-time environment for launching a new listening session.

Step-by-Step Guide
Real-time SangeetPitara Room Creation Screen showing Display Name, Room Name, and "+ Create private room".

Procedure Details

Step 1: Enter Your Display Name

Type your name (e.g., "Sam") in the first field so other listeners know who is hosting.

Step 2: Enter Room Title

Name your room (e.g., "Music Lounge", "Study Beats", or "Midnight Acoustic").

Step 3: Click "+ Create private room"

Your private room is generated immediately with an unambiguous 8-character code, and you enter as the Host.

💡 Host Privileges As Host, you have exclusive control to broadcast tab/device audio, review listener requests, assign Co-hosts, lock the room, or end the session.

Joining Room

Step-by-step procedure and real-time environment for connecting to an existing room.

Step-by-Step Guide
Real-time SangeetPitara Room Joining Screen showing the 8-character code input and Enter Room button.

Procedure Details

Step 1: Enter 8-Character Room Code

Paste or type the code (e.g. 3HLYQKQT) sent by your friend. Hyphens, spaces, and lowercase letters are automatically cleaned.

Step 2: Enter Your Display Name

Type your nickname so the host and other listeners see you in the active room roster.

Step 3: Click "Enter room"

You are immediately connected and synchronized with whatever audio is playing in the room.

💡 Direct Invite Links If your friend shared a direct link (e.g. ?room=3HLYQKQT), the room code is pre-filled automatically when you open the page!

Host Room & Audio Sharing

Detailed real-time environments for sharing audio on Windows Desktop, Android Phone, and MP3 files.

Audio Guide
Windows 11 / 10 Chrome screen share modal showing the critical "Also share tab audio" checkbox.

Desktop Procedure Details

Step 1: Click "Share audio"

In the Room tab, click the orange button. Windows Chrome / Edge pops up the screen-sharing picker.

⚠️ Step 2: TICK "Also share tab audio" (MANDATORY)

CRITICAL: In the bottom-left corner of the dialog, check "Also share tab audio" (or "Share system audio"). If left unchecked, Chrome streams only silent video!

Step 3: Select Music Tab or Screen

Under Chrome Tab, pick YouTube or Spotify. Or choose Entire Screen if playing from a desktop app.

Step 4: Click "Share"

The retro cassette reels spin and high-fidelity lossless sound streams live to everyone.

Step 1: Tap "Share device audio"
Step 2: Scope = "Entire screen" ➔ "Start"
Real-time Android Mobile screenshots showing "Share device audio" and "Entire screen" casting prompt.

Mobile Procedure Details

Step 1: Open SangeetPitara Android App

Join your room and tap the orange Share device audio button.

⚠️ Step 2: Set Scope to "Entire screen" (CRITICAL)

When Android displays "Start recording or casting with SangeetPitara?", set Scope to Entire screen (not a single app) so background music is captured!

Step 3: Tap Blue "Start" Button

Tap Start, then switch to Spotify, YouTube Music, or any music app. All audio streams live to the room!

📱 Using Phone Browser without the App? Tap "Share audio file" to stream any local MP3/WAV/FLAC track saved on your phone directly from your browser!
Real-time SangeetPitara Cassette Deck showing "Share audio file" button.

Local File Procedure Details

Step 1: Click "Share audio file"

Works on both Desktop and Mobile Phone browsers without requiring any screen capture permissions.

Step 2: Pick Local Music Track

Select any MP3, WAV, AAC, or FLAC track from your computer or phone's downloads folder.

Step 3: Instant Playback

The cassette reels spin in real time and everyone in the room listens together with zero re-encoding loss.

Inviting Users

Step-by-step procedure and real-time environment for sharing room links and managing participants.

Invite Guide
Real-time SangeetPitara People tab showing the room code, Copy Link button, and participant roster.

Procedure Details

Step 1: Open the People Tab

Click on the "People" tab in the room header.

Step 2: Click "Copy link"

Click the "Copy link" button to copy the direct URL with the 8-character room code to your clipboard.

Step 3: Send to Friends

Send via WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, or SMS. Anyone clicking the link joins your room immediately without an account.

Step 4: Promote Co-Hosts or Lock Room

Promote trusted listeners to Co-host to share audio control, or click "Lock room" to prevent uninvited guests.

Other Feature

Real-time environments for Chat, People roster, Help & Health diagnostics, and Studio Themes.

Features

💬 Chat Section

• Floating Reactions: Tap ❤️, 🔥, 👏, 🎧 for real-time animated emojis.
• Voice Notes: Hold the microphone button to record and send voice clips.
• Text Messages: Chat without interrupting live playback.

👥 People Section

• Active Roster: See all connected listeners.
• Role Badges: Distinguish Host, Co-hosts, and Listeners.
• Moderation: Remove disruptive listeners in one click.

🩺 Help & Health Section

• LIVE Status: Green badge confirms synchronized playback.
• Stream Health: Jitter buffer delay and packet loss telemetry.
• Save Diagnostics: Export anonymous debug logs.

🎨 Studio Themes

• 5 Themes: Dark, Light, Forest, Brass, and Rose.
• Topbar Switcher: Change theme instantly.
• Black Header: High contrast on all 5 themes.
