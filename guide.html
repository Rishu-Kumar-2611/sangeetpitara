Share Audio File (Any Device)
  • 1 Click "Share audio"

    On the Room tab, tap the orange button. Chrome / Edge will pop up the native share dialog.

  • 2 ⚠️ TICK "Also share tab audio"

    CRITICAL: Check the box at the bottom-left! If left unchecked, the browser sends only silent video without music.

  • 3 Pick the Music Tab or Screen

    Choose YouTube, Spotify, or "Entire Screen" if playing from a desktop app.

  • 4 Click "Share"

    Audio is captured in lossless high-fidelity PCM16 and streams live to everyone.

💡 Why the checkbox matters Browsers protect privacy by muting screen shares by default. Ticking "Also share tab audio" gives SangeetPitara permission to stream pure digital sound!
  • 1 Tap "Share device audio"

    In the SangeetPitara Android app, tap the orange Share device audio button in the room.

  • 2 ⚠️ Set Scope to "Entire screen"

    When Android asks "Start recording or casting with SangeetPitara?", ensure Scope is set to Entire screen (not a single app) so background music is captured!

  • 3 Tap "Start"

    Tap the blue Start button. Android now captures device audio playback and streams it directly to the room.

  • 4 Open Your Music App

    Switch to Spotify, YouTube Music, or any audio player. Everyone in the room will hear your music live in sync!

📱 In a Phone Browser? If you are using a mobile browser without the app, tap "Share audio file" to stream any local MP3/WAV/FLAC track from your phone storage!
  • 1 Click "Share audio file"

    Works on both Desktop and Mobile Phone browsers without needing any screen capture permissions!

  • 2 Select Local Song

    Pick any MP3, WAV, AAC, or FLAC track from your computer or phone's downloads/music folder.

  • 3 Instant Synchronized Stream

    The cassette visualizer will spin and all room participants will listen together.

⚡ Zero Quality Loss Direct file streaming broadcasts native audio frames with zero re-encoding artifacts.
