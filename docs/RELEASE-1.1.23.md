# SangeetPitara 1.1.23

## Voice messages pause the music

When you play a voice message in Chat (website or Android app), the room music
on your device goes quiet while it plays and comes back by itself when it
ends or you pause it. The room keeps playing live for everyone else, so the
music resumes at the live point. If you are the one sharing your phone's
music, your music app is not paused, because that would stop it for the whole
room.

## Light theme by default

The website and the Android app now start in the light theme. Anyone who
explicitly chose Dark keeps it; everyone else gets Light. The browser bar
colour and app install colours follow.

## Unread messages on the Chat tab

When someone else sends a message while you are not on the Chat tab, a green
count appears after "Chat" (website: a round bubble; Android: "Chat ●2"). It
clears as soon as you open Chat. Your own messages and the earlier history
shown when you join never count. Screen readers announce "Chat, 2 unread".

## Compatibility

Older apps keep working. Version 1.1.23 / versionCode 10123 / shared asset
cache 20261109-light1. No database migration.
