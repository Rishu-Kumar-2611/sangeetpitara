# RoomSound terms of service (deployment template)

> This is an implementation-ready template, not legal advice. Replace the
> bracketed details and obtain legal review before public launch.

## Service

RoomSound provides temporary or subscription-based private audio rooms. The
service does not promise that every device or application can be captured;
Android playback capture and browser tab capture depend on operating-system,
browser, application, and content restrictions.

## Acceptable use

Users may not use RoomSound to harass, impersonate, defraud, distribute malware,
evade access controls, violate privacy, or transmit content they do not have the
right to share. Users are responsible for complying with copyright, licensing,
privacy, and local law.

## Moderation

RoomSound may remove members, restrict accounts, end rooms, investigate reports,
and suspend or ban accounts when necessary to protect users or the service.
Reports and moderation actions are retained as described in the privacy notice.

## Payments

Paid plans, if enabled, renew through the configured payment processor. Plan
limits and cancellation rules are shown at checkout. Access can be suspended
for failed payment, fraud, abuse, or a material terms violation.

## Availability and limits

The initial production target is 100 concurrent users overall and 10 members per
room. Maintenance, network conditions, TURN relay availability, browser autoplay
rules, and third-party platform policies can interrupt a session.
