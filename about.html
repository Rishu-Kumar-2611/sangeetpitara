About SangeetPitara

SangeetPitara (“the treasure chest of music”) lets you listen together — even when you are not in the same room. One phone plays, everyone listens in sync on phone or laptop.

What it does

  • Create or join in one click — no account needed. Create a room, share a 6-character code or invite link.
  • Live, not recorded — audio is relayed live and never stored on disk.
  • Phone → Laptop — Android app captures device playback via MediaProjection/AudioPlaybackCapture; desktop Chrome/Edge can share tab/system audio via getDisplayMedia. Phone browsers can listen, chat, and request to play.
  • Host controls — approve/reject who plays, remove members, mute locally, chat and see who is listening.
  • Up to 10 listeners per room — perfect for family, friends, hostels, and small communities.
  • Bonus — Android app available at /downloads/SangeetPitara.apk

Why the Diya + Eighth Note?

The logo fuses a diya (light, warmth, togetherness) with an eighth note — music that lights up the room. That is the whole idea: your music, your people, one synced room.

Technology

Browser relay uses binary PCM16 over WebSocket (48 kHz mono) with a soft limiter, jitter-aware playout (AudioWorklet), and optional WebRTC/Direct path. Android uses MediaProjection foreground service + native WebRTC. No audio is written to storage. Health, metrics, and audit logs keep the service reliable. See GitHub repository for source if you self-host.

Who is behind it

Roadmap

Free during the habit-building phase (ad-supported). Premium subscriptions (longer sessions, larger rooms, cloud history) will launch after the community grows — early users will keep generous free limits.
