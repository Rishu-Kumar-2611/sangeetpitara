Privacy Policy

Effective 24 Oct 2024 · Updated 8 Nov 2026 · DG, India

SangeetPitara runs private live audio rooms at sangeet-pitaraa.onrender.com. Live music is relayed in memory. Voice messages are short recordings that are shared with room participants only when you tap Send. They are temporarily retained in bounded room history; Android uses private temporary files when recording and playing them. We collect only what is needed to keep rooms working.

Data we process

  • Display name + device guest ID in localStorage (no account needed).
  • Room membership, source requests, chat and live audio are held in memory. Voice messages and reply quotes are temporary room chat content, not a permanent message archive. Recordings may become unavailable as room history is trimmed or when a host/server restarts. Recipients can save or copy content they receive. When persistent storage is configured, room metadata, accounts and moderation records are stored in PostgreSQL. Guest-created rooms end when the server restarts.
  • Security: IP (X-Forwarded-For only behind trusted proxy), User-Agent, rate limits, redacted audit logs, Report/Block/Ban records.
  • Optional admin auth: email + hashed password and HttpOnly cookie roomsound_session (7–30 days).
  • Preferences and identity: ad consent, volume, a public guest ID and a private reconnect secret in browser localStorage or Android private app storage. The reconnect secret authenticates room recovery; do not share it.

Sharing & rights

Audio diagnostics stay in memory on your device until you choose Save audio diagnostics. The downloaded JSON contains a bounded recent history of technical measurements and fixed event types, without audio, screen content, names, chat, room codes, IP addresses, invite URLs or credentials. It is not automatically uploaded. Only share that file with support if you choose to do so.

Connections use TLS on the public service. Live PCM audio passes through our server and is not end-to-end encrypted. The application does not record audio.

We share only with providers to run the service (host, DB, optional TURN/Stripe, Google only after consent). Access/correction/deletion — email via Contact; 30-day reply. Not directed at under-13s.
