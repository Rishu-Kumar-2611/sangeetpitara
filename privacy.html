What we process

RoomSound processes account details, secure session hashes, room and chat data, moderation records, subscription metadata, WebRTC signaling, and operational security data needed to run the service.

Audio

RoomSound does not record or store room audio. Audio may pass through a configured TURN relay while a connection is active.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled by default. If sponsored placements are enabled, RoomSound asks for consent before loading the configured advertising provider and stores a local consent choice. Replace this template with the final regional privacy and consent details before enabling ads.
