Privacy Policy

Effective 24 Oct 2024 · Updated 24 Oct 2026 · DG, India · Contact · support@sangeet-pitaraa.onrender.com

SangeetPitara runs private live audio rooms at sangeet-pitaraa.onrender.com. Audio is relayed live in memory and never stored on disk. We collect only what is needed to keep rooms working.

Data we process

  • Display name + device guest ID in localStorage (no account needed).
  • Room code, roster, host role, source requests, ephemeral chat (200/100) and live PCM16/WebRTC signals — in memory, gone when room closes.
  • Security: IP (X-Forwarded-For only behind trusted proxy), User-Agent, rate limits, redacted audit logs, Report/Block/Ban records.
  • Optional admin auth: email + hashed password and HttpOnly cookie roomsound_session (7–30 days).
  • Preferences: ad consent, volume — in localStorage. (Rewarded-access gate is currently disabled; no expiry is stored.)

We share only with providers to run the service (host, DB, optional TURN/Stripe, Google only after consent). Access/correction/deletion — email via Contact; 30-day reply. Not directed at under-13s.
