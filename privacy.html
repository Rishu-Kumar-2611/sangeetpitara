Privacy

What we process

SangeetPitara processes room and chat metadata, temporary guest identifiers, moderation records, subscription metadata, WebRTC signaling, and operational security data needed to run the live listening service.

Audio

SangeetPitara never records or stores room audio on disk. Live audio frames are transiently relayed in memory through the SangeetPitara media relay server or a configured TURN relay only while a room source is actively streaming.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled by default. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this site or other sites. Users can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. SangeetPitara asks for consent before loading any configured advertising provider and stores a local consent choice.

Requests

For privacy, access, correction, export, or deletion inquiries, contact the SangeetPitara administrator via the official repository issue tracker or support channel.
