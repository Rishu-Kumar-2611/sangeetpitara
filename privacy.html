What we process

SangeetPitara processes account details, secure session hashes, room and chat data, moderation records, subscription metadata, WebRTC signaling, and operational security data needed to run the service.

Audio

SangeetPitara does not record or store room audio. Audio may pass through a configured TURN relay while a connection is active.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled by default. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this site or other sites. Users can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. SangeetPitara asks for consent before loading the configured advertising provider and stores a local consent choice. Replace this template with final regional privacy and consent details before enabling ads.
