    What these numbers mean

    Devices are approximate unique app/browser identities, not verified people. Clearing app storage or using another browser creates a new identity. Room time counts connected time across sessions.

    Listening time estimates audio sent by the cloud relay to listeners who have not paused. It does not prove the phone played it audibly. The source phone, direct WebRTC playback and offline nearby rooms are excluded. Queued audio may differ from actual playback.

    Tracking starts with this update. Daily buckets use UTC. Totals persist with PostgreSQL; local development memory resets on restart. Reports refresh on demand; recent activity is saved every 15 seconds.

    Only aggregate usage is shown. No chat content, audio recordings, device names, raw guest IDs or IP addresses are collected by these statistics.
