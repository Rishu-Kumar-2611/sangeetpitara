## 1. How to Create a Room

Starting a room is instant and requires no sign-up or installation on any device.

![Room Creation Interface](/C:/Users/DG/.gemini/antigravity/brain/63c30635-0c47-41ca-8074-fc0d46ba1ecc/guide_assets/01-create-room.png)

```
[1] Display Name Input (e.g. "Sam") ───► Type how you appear to others
[2] Room Name Input (e.g. "Music Lounge") ───► Title of your listening session
[3] "+ Create private room" Button ───► Click to launch your room!
```

### Steps:
1. **Enter Your Display Name**: Type the name you want friends to see.
2. **Name Your Room**: Pick a name (e.g., *Study Beats*, *Party Mix*).
3. **Click `+ Create private room`**: An 8-character unique room code is generated immediately and you enter as the **Host**.

## 2. How to Join a Room

Joining an existing session takes only a few seconds.

![Room Joining Interface](/C:/Users/DG/.gemini/antigravity/brain/63c30635-0c47-41ca-8074-fc0d46ba1ecc/guide_assets/02-join-room.png)

### Steps:

> [!NOTE]

## 3. How to Share Audio (In-Depth)

Sharing high-fidelity digital audio differs slightly between **Desktop** and **Mobile** because operating systems manage audio permissions differently.

---

### A. Desktop Browser (Chrome / Edge)

When you share music from YouTube, Spotify Web, SoundCloud, or your desktop:

![Desktop Browser Screen and Audio Share Dialog](/C:/Users/DG/.gemini/antigravity/brain/63c30635-0c47-41ca-8074-fc0d46ba1ecc/guide_assets/03b-desktop-share-dialog.svg)

#### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. In the **Room** tab, click the orange **`Share audio`** button.
2. Chrome / Edge will pop up the **"Choose what to share"** dialog.
3. **Select your tab or screen**:
   - Choose **Chrome Tab** to share music from YouTube or Spotify Web.
   - Choose **Entire Screen** if you are playing audio from a desktop app (e.g., Spotify desktop or VLC).
4. **⚠️ CRITICAL: Check the Audio Box!**
   - On the **Chrome Tab** panel: Look at the bottom-left corner and ensure **`Also share tab audio`** is **ticked**.
   - On the **Entire Screen** panel: Ensure **`Share system audio`** is **ticked**.
   > [!WARNING]
   > If this checkbox is unchecked, Chrome shares only silent video! Ticking this box is mandatory for your listeners to hear sound.
5. Click **`Share`**. The vintage cassette reels will spin in real time and everyone in the room will hear the stream.

---

### B. Mobile Phone (Android App & Casting)

Mobile operating systems (Android and iOS) block web browsers from capturing audio playback from other apps (like Spotify or YouTube Music). To stream background music from your phone, use the **SangeetPitara Android App**.

#### Real Android Interfaces:

| Step 1: In the SangeetPitara App | Step 2: Android System Permission |
| :---: | :---: |
| ![SangeetPitara Mobile App Screen](/C:/Users/DG/.gemini/antigravity/brain/63c30635-0c47-41ca-8074-fc0d46ba1ecc/guide_assets/03c-mobile-real-room.jpg) | ![Android Entire Screen Casting Dialog](/C:/Users/DG/.gemini/antigravity/brain/63c30635-0c47-41ca-8074-fc0d46ba1ecc/guide_assets/03d-mobile-real-dialog.jpg) |
| ➔ Tap orange **`Share device audio`** | ➔ Set **Scope** to **`Entire screen`** and tap **`Start`** |

#### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Open the **SangeetPitara Android App** and join your room.
2. Tap the orange **`Share device audio`** button.
3. Android will display the system prompt: **"Start recording or casting with SangeetPitara?"**
4. **⚠️ CRITICAL: Ensure Scope is "Entire screen"!**
   - Tap the **Scope** dropdown and select **`Entire screen`** (do not select a single app).
   - This allows Android's audio capture engine to stream background music from Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, or your local player.
5. Tap the blue **`Start`** button.
6. Switch to your music app. All audio now streams live to everyone in the room in real time!

> [!TIP]
> **Using a phone browser without the app?**
> Simply tap **`Share audio file`** on the room page to stream any MP3/WAV/FLAC track saved on your phone directly from your browser!

### C. Sharing Local Audio Files (MP3 / WAV / FLAC)

Need to play a downloaded track or local song without sharing your screen?

![Local Audio File Sharing Button](/C:/Users/DG/.gemini/antigravity/brain/63c30635-0c47-41ca-8074-fc0d46ba1ecc/guide_assets/03-share-audio.png)

1. On **any device** (Desktop, Android phone, iPhone), click **`Share audio file`**.
2. Select any `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.aac`, or `.flac` track stored on your device.
3. The track streams directly to the room with zero re-encoding loss.

## 4. Essential Room Features

SangeetPitara includes intuitive room tools that are always accessible in one tap:

| Feature | Where it is | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **💬 Chat & Voice Notes** | `Chat` tab | Send messages, float one-tap emoji reactions (❤️, 🔥, 👏, 🎧), or hold the **mic button** to record and drop voice notes without interrupting music playback. |
| **🩺 Health Diagnostics** | `Health` tab | View real-time stream quality, jitter buffer latency, and packet loss telemetry. Green dot indicates optimal playback. |
| **🎨 5 Studio Themes** | Top-right `THEME` | Switch instantly between **Dark**, **Light**, **Forest**, **Brass**, and **Rose**. All themes feature high-contrast solid black tab headers. |
| **👥 People & Invites** | `People` tab | View active members, host badges, and click **`Copy link`** to share the room URL. |
