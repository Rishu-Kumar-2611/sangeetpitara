Synchronized Audio Room Guide Version 1.1.24 · Desktop & Mobile

1 How to Create a Room (Desktop & Mobile)

Start a new listening session as the Host in just two clicks. No registration or account required.

1. Enter Your Display Name

Type your name (e.g., "Sam") in the first field so other listeners know who is hosting.

2. Enter a Room Name

Name the session (e.g., "Music Lounge" or "Study Beats").

3. Click "+ Create private room"

Your private room is generated instantly with an 8-character code and you enter the room as Host.

2 How to Join a Room (Desktop & Mobile)

Connect to a live listening room using an 8-character code or direct invite link.

1. Enter the 8-Character Room Code

Paste the code (e.g. 3HLYQKQT) sent by your friend. Spaces and hyphens are automatically cleaned.

2. Enter Your Display Name & Click "Enter room"

Type your nickname and click Enter room to immediately sync into the live audio stream.

3A How to Share Audio — Desktop Browser (Windows Chrome / Edge)

When sharing sound from YouTube, Spotify Web, SoundCloud, or desktop apps on a Windows PC:

⚠️ STEP 1: TICK "Also share tab audio" (MANDATORY)

CRITICAL: In the bottom-left corner of the Chrome dialog, you must check the box for "Also share tab audio" (or "Share system audio"). If unchecked, Chrome only streams silent video without sound!

STEP 2: Select the Music Tab or Screen

Under Chrome Tab, pick YouTube or Spotify. Or switch to Entire Screen if playing from a desktop player.

STEP 3: Click "Share"

Click the blue Share button. The cassette reels spin and high-fidelity sound streams live to everyone.

3B How to Share Audio — Mobile Phone (Android App)

Mobile web browsers cannot capture background audio from other apps. To stream phone audio, use the SangeetPitara Android App:

Step 1: Tap "Share device audio"
Step 2: Scope = "Entire screen" ➔ Tap "Start"
1. Tap the Orange "Share device audio" Button

Inside your room in the Android app, tap Share device audio.

⚠️ 2. Set Scope to "Entire screen" (CRITICAL)

When Android asks "Start recording or casting with SangeetPitara?", tap the Scope selector and choose Entire screen (not a single app) so background music is captured!

3. Tap the Blue "Start" Button & Switch to Your Music App

Tap Start. Now open Spotify, YouTube Music, or any media app. Everyone in the room hears your music in sync!

💡 In a Phone Browser without the App?

Tap "Share audio file" in your phone browser to stream local MP3/WAV/FLAC files from phone storage without needing any app!
